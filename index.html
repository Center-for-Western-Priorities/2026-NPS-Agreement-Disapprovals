    Source and method

    Records from NPS FAST financial assistance tracking, status as of September 9, 2026. Only requests still carrying a Disapproved status are shown; seven from the same August 7 batch have since moved to another status and are excluded.

    Park markers use NPS Land Resources Division boundary centroids. Regional offices, national programs, and inventory and monitoring networks support groups of parks rather than one; each is drawn as a square at the office address NPS publishes for it. Every marker sits at its true coordinate, and markers hold a constant size as you zoom, so zooming pulls overlapping locations apart.
